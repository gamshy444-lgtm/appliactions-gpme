# Partie 2 — images à générer (style 1 : film réaliste)

Format **vertical 9:16**, aucun texte dans l'image. Colle chaque description telle quelle (ChatGPT, Canva, Midjourney…).
Pour que le garçon et la maison restent les mêmes d'une image à l'autre, chaque description répète les mêmes détails.

Personnage : *a 17-year-old boy with short dark messy hair, wearing a dark grey hoodie*.
Maison : *blue-grey walls, white wooden doors, dark wooden floor*.
Style commun (à garder à la fin de chaque description) :
*Cinematic realistic film still, vertical 9:16, thriller, moody but clearly visible details, shallow depth of field, film grain, teal and amber tones. No text, no words, no logos.*

## Début A (il ouvre)
**A1 — le couloir vide**
A bedroom door half open onto an empty dark hallway at 2am, blue-grey walls, white wooden doors, dark wooden floor, a single warm wall lamp glowing at the end of the hallway, a wooden staircase banister. Seen from inside the bedroom. + style commun

**A2 — la photo par terre**
An old family photograph lying face up on the dark wooden floor of a hallway at night, lit by a warm wall lamp, seen from above at a slight angle. + style commun

## Début B (il se cache)
**B1 — la porte et les pieds**
A closed white bedroom door seen from inside a dark bedroom at 2am, a thin line of warm light under the door with the shadows of two feet standing on the other side, the door handle in focus. + style commun

**B2 — la photo glissée**
A photograph being slid under a closed white bedroom door onto the dark wooden floor, warm light coming from under the door, close-up at floor level. + style commun

## Suite commune
